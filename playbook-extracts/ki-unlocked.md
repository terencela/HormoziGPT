## 2. Pricing structure and price-raise approach

**What is already right**

- Prices exkl. MwSt., in CHF, by format — clear by Swiss B2B standards.
- Workshop is productised (Pricing play: make it easy to say yes).
- Autopilot is Festpreis plus monthly, quoted after a free diagnosis (filters tyre-kickers, protects margin).
- Head of AI is sold as days per month, not as a vague retainer.

**What to tighten**

- **Annual workshop or mandate prepay** (Pricing play 5): e.g. four open-date seats for one firm, or 12 months Head of AI billed annually with a modest discount. `[PLATZHALTER: Discount]`
- **Round numbers** — already clean (1'900, 3'900, 6'900, 390). Keep it that way.
- **Annual adjustment clause** in mandates and Autopilot operating fees (play 4 plus Price Raise). Once a year, after a short value reminder, not a silent slide.
- **Don't grandfather early friends forever.** If airport-network or "first ten firms" deals exist, put an end date in writing.
- **VAT / invoicing:** keep QR-bill / bank transfer easy. Confirm any card-fee handling.
- **Ultra-high anchor:** a "Kontrollturm" package (several Autopilots + 8-day Head of AI + Champions) priced visibly above the parts, for Geschäftsleitung that wants one signature.

**When to raise**

- Workshop prices can stay still while you are filling open dates and collecting proof; raise when close rate on the Halbtag is consistently easy (Hormozi: very high close rates often mean you are under-priced) *and* the room is full.
- Autopilot operating fees: raise for new customers first when the Company Brain or connector work has clearly expanded.
- Head of AI: publish a grid (2 / 4 / 8 days) so the conversation does not restart from zero every time.

**RAISE letter (German skeleton, for mandates / Autopilot-Betrieb)**

- **R:** "In den letzten zwölf Monaten: `[Zahl Autopiloten / Freigaben / gemessene Stunden oder Durchlaufzeit / Champions]`."
- **A:** "Ab `[Datum]` gilt für `[Mandat / Betrieb]` `[alt]` → `[neu]`, exkl. MwSt."

## 5. Lead-nurture sequence (Potenzial-Check and workshop leads)

The Check is the "demo." Workshops and Autopilot Offerten are what it should convert to. Apply the four pillars.

**Availability.** Offer early, lunch, and late-afternoon slots; some open Friday mornings. 15-minute increments. Self-scheduling. For open workshops, show remaining seats honestly.

**Speed.** Auto-confirm in one minute. A personal mail from Terence or a colleague the same half-day. Aim to place the Check inside 72 hours, or immediately after a booked trip.

**Personalisation.** Ask: Branche, Firmengrösse, Stufe (Tool already in use?), one process that hurts, data constraint. Use that in every follow-up. Architektur / Planung can be asked whether Honorarangebote are the pain (Leistera) or team capability (workshop) — never both in the first sentence.

**Volume.** Don't stop after one unanswered mail. Softened cadence below.

## 6. Closing script for the Potenzial-Check

**Goal.** A recommended next step and a date, or a clean no. Not a same-day signature on an 8-day mandate unless they came for that and the scope is already clear.

### Frame (first two minutes)

> Ziel in 30 Minuten: ein gemeinsames Bild, auf welcher Stufe Sie stehen, und ein nächster Schritt, der in Wochen sichtbar ist — nicht in einem Jahr. Am Ende steht typischerweise: ein Workshop-Format, ein Autopilot-Umfang zum Offerieren, ein späterer Zeitpunkt, oder dass wir nicht die richtigen sind.

### Diagnosis

- What already runs (tools, licences, unofficial use).
- Where the hours or the errors sit.
- Who would approve, who would use, who in IT / Datenschutz must say yes.
- Data constraints (CH/EU, professional secrecy for Treuhand/Kanzlei/Praxis).
- Whether they need capability (workshop / Champions), a process (Autopilot), or leadership (Head of AI) — often a sequence, not a bundle on day one.

### All-purpose questions

> Was müsste nach 30 Tagen oder nach 4 Wochen wahr sein, damit Sie sagen: das hat sich gelohnt?

> Wenn wir genau den Ablauf `[X]` in Ihren Systemen in 4 Wochen live hätten, mit Freigabe durch `[Person]` — wäre das der richtige erste Schritt, oder fehlt etwas anderes?

> Auf einer Skala von 1 bis 10: wie klar ist der nächste Schritt? Was fehlte für eine 9?

### Objection: data security

> Richtig, das klären wir vor dem Auftrag, nicht danach. Grundsatz: Daten in der Schweiz oder in der EU, wie vereinbart; ein Mensch gibt frei; wir trainieren nicht frei mit Ihrer Akte an öffentlichen Modellen. Es gibt einen Auftragsverarbeitungsvertrag und eine Liste der Systeme. Wenn Sie in Treuhand, Medizin oder einer Kanzlei sind, sagen Sie uns die Berufsgeheimnis-Grenze zuerst — dann entscheiden wir, ob und wie ein Autopilot überhaupt zulässig ist.

`[PLATZHALTER: AVV, Unterauftragsverarbeiter, Hosting-Orte]`

### Objection: "Wir nutzen bereits ChatGPT / Copilot."

> Dann sind Sie typischerweise bei Stufe 1. Das ist der häufigste Startpunkt. Die Frage ist nicht «haben Sie ein Tool?», sondern: arbeitet das ganze Team damit an echten Aufgaben, mit Regeln, und läuft schon ein Ablauf ohne dass jemand ihn jedes Mal neu erfindet? Wenn ja, reden wir über Autopilot oder Leitung. Wenn nein, ist der Workshop der kürzere Weg als noch ein Tool.

### Objection: price (workshop)

> Der Halbtag ist CHF 3'900 exkl. MwSt. für das Team, bis 15 Personen, inkl. Vorgespräch und Check-in nach 30 Tagen. Wenn Sie zuerst mit zwei, drei Personen prüfen wollen: offener Termin für CHF 390. Wenn der Preis die Hürde ist, ist oft der Umfang die eigentliche Frage — Impuls für die Geschäftsleitung für 1'900, nicht gleich der ganze Tag.

### Objection: price (Autopilot / Head of AI)

> Die Einrichtung ist ein Festpreis, den Sie vor dem Start schriftlich haben. Der Betrieb ist ein fixer Monatsbetrag, ohne Überraschungen pro Token. Head of AI rechnen wir in Tagen pro Monat, 2, 4 oder 8. Wir verkleinern lieber den ersten Ablauf, als einen unklaren Rabatt auf einen unklaren Umfang zu geben.

### Objection: time / "wir sind gerade in der Hochsaison"

> Dann ist ein Impuls oder ein einzelner Autopilot oft besser als ein grosses Programm. Welches Fenster nach `[Peak]` ist realistisch? Wir legen den Setup-Start dorthin und nutzen die 30 Minuten heute, um den Ablauf und die Offerte festzuhalten — ohne dass Ihr Team diese Woche etwas umstellen muss.

### Objection: "Ich muss die Geschäftsleitung / IT / Datenschutz fragen."

## 7. Proof and case-study checklist

You already have **authority proof** (airport role, stages, Forbes 30 Under 30, 2× TEDx, prior firms named on the site). You still need **customer-result proof** that matches the buyer's industry.

### Collect every week

- Workshop: anonymous or named quotes, plus a behavioural metric you already list internally (how many people use KI weekly; how many Arbeitsmuster were reused; how long one process took before/after). The Champions page already names those metrics — start measuring them.
- Autopilot: number of approvals, time in the process, error/correction rate, hours returned *if measured*.
- A raw 20-second clip from a workshop (participants' permission).
- Freshness: a result from this quarter beats a 2023 logo.

### Hierarchy, applied

- A Treuhand office watching another Treuhand story beats a generic "KMU" quote.
- A live Autopilot demo beats a slide about Autopiloten.
- "Freigabe durch die Sachbearbeiterin, 12 Wochen, `[gemessene Zahl]`" beats "deutlich effizienter."
- Google review, written LinkedIn recommendation, or conference quote beats a line Terence wrote about himself.
- The 38-hours example on the Autopilot page: `[PLATZHALTER: entweder Quelle / Erlaubnis, oder klar als Rechenbeispiel gekennzeichnet lassen]`

### Case-study skeleton

Branche, Grösse, Ausgangsstufe, gekauftes Angebot, Zeitraum, was der Mensch weiter freigibt, gemessene Zahlen, wörtliches Zitat mit Erlaubnis, Datum, ein Screen oder ein Vorher/Nachher am Prozess — keine Kundendaten im Bild.

### Do not

- Imply UBS, Credit Suisse, PwC or Flughafen Zürich are KI-Unlocked case studies if they are biography / past employment / speaking contexts.
- Reuse Leistera product metrics as agency proof, or vice versa.

## 8. Retention and LTV expansion

The ladder **is** the LTV design, if you operate it on purpose.

**Activation points**

- Workshop: every attendee has one Arbeitsmuster used again in week 1; 30-day check-in actually happens (it is already sold — run it).
- Autopilot: first live approval in production; tenth approval; first monthly report discussed with the process owner.
- Head of AI: first steering meeting with Geschäftsleitung; first Champion nominated; first new Autopilot logged from Champion input.

**Onboarding / 30-day check-in.** Treat it as retention, not a courtesy. Agenda: what is in use, what died, which process is now Autopilot-ready.

**Community / Champions.** The Champions programme is Hormozi's "community linking" in Swiss clothes. Price it clearly; don't let it become an unpaid extra that only you remember.

**Annual options.** Prepay a year of Head of AI or Autopilot operating fees; pre-buy a Serie of workshops for several departments.

**Correct or fire.** A client who wants fully autonomous answers on confidential files with no approver is a brand risk. Walk away.

**Crazy 8, in likely order**

1. Raise mandate / operating fees for *new* clients as delivery gets tighter.
2. Cut delivery cost: productised Autopilot recipes per industry (the site already has Treuhand, Praxen, Kanzleien, Handwerk, Architektur).
3. More workshops in the same firm (other departments) — already foreshadowed by the Serie.
4. Cross-sell Autopilot after workshop; Head of AI after the first Autopilot; GEO as a bolt-on for firms that care about inbound; Leistera only for Planungsbüros with a document problem.
5. More volume (more Autopilots, more days).
6. Higher quality / wider scope (Company Brain across all Autopilots, Kontrollturm package).
7. Downsell: Impuls instead of a full day; 2 days of Head of AI instead of 8; one Autopilot instead of three.
8. A lighter "only Richtlinie plus Check-in" package if the alternative is losing them entirely.

**Fast Cash, here, is not a mystery product.** The warm list is past workshop attendees and Check no-shows who said "later." Quarterly, offer a capped, dated thing: a private Halbtag for alumni, a Champions kick-off, or a two-Autopilot bundle with a real seat limit. Run the 7-day sequence only to that list.

## 9. Thirty-day plan (Fast Cash, adapted)

Assumption: you already have published offers and some warm pipeline. The 30 days are for **cash and proof**, not for inventing a new brand.

### Days 1–7 — Inventory and rules

- Export the warm list: Checks not booked, Checks done but not bought, workshop alumni, keynote leads, tool-download mails. `[PLATZHALTER: tatsächliche Listenquelle / CRM]`
- Cap something real: e.g. `[3]` Autopilot setups starting next month, or `[1]` open Halbtag with remaining seats.
- Decide one bonus that costs you time, not margin (30-day transfer call; Richtlinie review).
- Freeze claim rules (hosting language; 38-hour example; logo use).
- Put the 30-day workshop check-in on the calendar for every recent workshop — that call is also a sales conversation for Autopilot, if earned.

### Days 8–14 — Tease the warm list

- Personal mail or WhatsApp: "something concrete next week" is fine if the thing is real.
- Announcement: either the open workshop date (CHF 390) or "next Autopilot cohort, Festpreis after a 30-minute Check, start in `[month]`."
- LinkedIn: hooks 1, 14–16, 21, 27. Manual comments, no automation theatre.

### Days 15–24 — Conversations

- Fill the Check calendar. Same-day Offerten.
- For alumni: skip the philosophy, ask which process is ready (Pillar III personalisation).
- Fill the open workshop first if seats are empty — it is the closest thing to a clean cash product you already have.

### Days 25–30 — Close the window and deliver

- "Open date is full" or "next Autopilot starts are `[month]`" — only if true.
- Run every overdue 30-day check-in.
- File objections and one new proof item, even if it is only an anonymised quote.

**Don't:** discount the Halbtag in public; promise Autopilots in less than you can deliver; blast a 10x "ultra" offer to a cold list; mix Leistera pricing into KI Unlocked invoices.

## 10. Highest-leverage moves (summary)

1. **Climb the ladder on purpose.** Every workshop gets a 30-day check-in; every good check-in gets an Autopilot Offerte; Autopilot clients hear about Champions and Head of AI at a fixed point, not when things go quiet.
2. **Turn the Potenzial-Check into a show-rate machine.** Instant booking, 72-hour slots, human reminders, same-day written next step.
3. **Replace illustrative proof with permissioned proof.** Measure the metrics you already list (use, patterns reused, process time). Clarify or source the "38 Stunden" example before it is repeated in ads.
4. **Publish a price band for Head of AI and, if honest, an Autopilot «from» range.** The workshop grid already converts; the two higher rungs still ask the buyer to walk in blind.
5. **Once a quarter, run a capped offer to alumni and warm Checks only** (open date, private Halbtag, or a two-process Autopilot start). That is the Fast Cash play that fits this business.
